About OVANCO

An AI-powered ovarian cancer screening platform built at the intersection of bioengineering, deep learning, and clinical need.

The problem

Ovarian cancer is one of the most lethal gynaecological cancers — largely because it is frequently diagnosed at advanced stages. Accurate interpretation of ultrasound imaging requires specialist expertise not always accessible in resource-limited settings. OVANCO bridges that gap with AI-assisted first-pass screening.

How it works

OVANCO uses one deep learning model with GradCAM visualization on every uploaded ultrasound scan:

  • Classification model — EfficientNetB3 pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on 1,469 clinically annotated ovarian ultrasound images (MMOTU dataset). Trained in two phases: head-only training followed by top-layer fine-tuning. Test accuracy: 76.68% · AUC: 0.905.
  • GradCAM Visualization — Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping using GradientTape to compute gradient-based saliency maps, highlighting the specific image regions that drove the classification decision. Red/Orange = high attention; Blue = low attention.

Dataset

Trained on the MMOTU (Multi-Modality Ovarian Tumor Ultrasound) dataset — 1,469 clinically annotated 2D ultrasound images from Beijing Shijitan Hospital, Capital Medical University, annotated by 27 gynaecology experts. Published: Zhao et al., IEEE 2022.
